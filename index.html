The Story

Where Heritage
Meets Craft

This is Siphokazi is rooted in the tradition of South African beadwork — a living art form passed through generations. Each piece is handcrafted with intention, carrying the language of pattern and color that defines our cultural identity.

Beyond creating beautiful beadwork, Siphokazi is committed to passing this knowledge forward through hands-on training, empowering new artisans to carry the craft into the future.
