# Backlink & Citation Opportunities — celestinoenterprise.com

**Prepared:** 2026-09-02 · companion to `content-gap-analysis.md` (assets referenced below map to its numbered pieces)

## Method & limitations

- Opportunities were identified through web search of Virginia/Fredericksburg/Richmond business, economic-development, procurement, media, academic, and security-community sites plus standard vendor/directory channels. Pages were not fetched (proxy blocked); membership costs, submission forms and dofollow/nofollow status were taken from snippets and should be confirmed before outreach.
- **Authority** is a qualitative estimate (High / Med / Low) of domain trust, not a tool metric. **Difficulty** reflects effort + gatekeeping. **Relationship required** is honest about what must exist first (membership, certification, partnership, client story).
- Many government/association links are nofollow or directory-style. They still matter for local relevance, NAP consistency, procurement discovery, and AI-search entity grounding. Do not chase only dofollow.
- Nothing here should be pursued with paid placements disguised as editorial, reciprocal link schemes, or claims (SWaM, partner tiers, awards) that are not real.

## Prerequisites before any outreach

1. Confirm/restore Virginia SCC entity status (a snippet showed INACTIVE). Directories and reporters check it.
2. Lock the canonical NAP: `Celestino Enterprise LLC · 13329 Fredericksburg Tpke, Woodford, VA 22580 · (202) 650-8607 (verify primary number; consider a 540/804 local line for GBP)`.
3. Publish: Trust Center, Government capabilities page (HTML + PDF), one case study, one or two checklists, team/author bios.
4. Set up a shared outreach tracker (target, contact, date, status, URL earned, follow/nofollow).

## NEVER list

- Never buy links, sponsored posts labeled as editorial, or "guest post packages"; never join link exchanges or private blog networks.
- Never claim SWaM, SBA 8(a)/HUBZone/WOSB/SDVOSB, SOC 2, ISO 27001, CMMC RPO/C3PAO, or vendor partner tiers that are not verifiably held.
- Never list in vendor partner directories without an active agreement (Microsoft, Datto/Kaseya, ConnectWise, Fortinet, etc.).
- Never create fake reviews on Google, Clutch, BBB, Yelp; never incentivize reviews in ways those platforms prohibit.
- Never mass-submit to low-quality "business directories" or bookmarking sites; keep citations to platforms a real customer or procurement officer would use.
- Never publish doorway/town pages or scaled AI-generated articles to attract links (Google scaled-content and site-reputation abuse policies).
- Never imply clients (hospital systems, counties, bases) that are not clients; never use logos without written permission.
- Never use the "award winning" claim without naming the award and year.
- Never pay for placement on "Top MSPs in Virginia" lists without a visible "sponsored" label.
- Never disclose client security details in case studies without written approval and redaction.
