## Method & limitations

- Sources: ~60 web searches (SERP snippets for Fredericksburg/Richmond/Stafford/Spotsylvania MSP queries, competitor URL inventories, Google Search Central guidance, Virginia procurement/SBSD/VITA pages), plus the current celestinoenterprise.com service list and prior knowledge of MSP/MSSP search behavior.
- Direct page fetches to most external domains were blocked by the proxy; competitor pages were inferred from indexed URLs, titles, and snippets — not crawled.
- **No numeric search volumes are asserted as facts.** Demand is expressed as tiers: **High** (national head term, thousands/mo est.), **Med** (hundreds/mo est. nationally, or strong local pack behavior), **Low** (tens/mo est., long-tail or local-modified), **Local-only** (volume mostly invisible in tools; appears as GBP/map-pack and "near me" demand). Validate with GSC after launch and a paid keyword tool (Semrush/Ahrefs) before committing to targets.
- "Questions" are People-Also-Ask-style phrasings compiled from SERP features observed in snippets plus known PAA patterns for the MSP category; they are not scraped verbatim.
- Geographic modifiers: only use where a page has genuine local proof (address, service radius, client sector, on-site capability). Do not generate one thin page per town (see competitive-analysis.md on doorway-page risk).
