# HOW LOCAL DIGITAL MARKETING ACTUALLY WORKS (2026)

Use this as fact. Never contradict it with generic advice.

## Google Business Profile (GBP) — the #1 lead source for local businesses
- Local pack ranking = Relevance + Distance + Prominence. You cannot change distance. You can change relevance (categories, services, description, posts, keywords in reviews) and prominence (review count, review velocity, review replies, photos, citations, website authority).
- Primary category is the single biggest ranking lever. Secondary categories add relevance for more searches.
- Every service should be listed under Services with a description. Products can be used for packages.
- Reviews: velocity matters more than total. 3 to 5 new reviews per month beats 50 old ones. Reviews that mention the service and city ("best roofer in Fredericksburg") boost relevance. Reply to every review within 24 hours using the service keyword naturally. Never buy or fake reviews. Never gate reviews (only asking happy customers) in a way that violates Google policy. Ask every customer, make it a one-tap link.
- Photos: upload 2 to 4 real, geotagged job photos per week. Real work, real people, real trucks. Stock photos hurt trust.
- GBP Posts: 1 to 2 per week. Types: Update, Offer, Event. Structure: hook line, 1 to 2 sentences of value or proof, CTA with the phone number. Include the city and service. Posts expire from view fast, so consistency beats length.
- Q&A: seed 5 to 10 real questions with answers. Owners can post and answer their own.
- NAP (Name, Address, Phone) must match exactly across website, GBP, Facebook, Yelp, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Nextdoor.
- Booking / messaging turned on. Answer within minutes or turn it off.
- Website link should point to a page whose title and H1 match the primary category and city.
- Track: calls, direction requests, website clicks, and search terms in GBP Insights. Use UTM links on the website URL.

## Local SEO (website)
- One page per core service, one page per city served. Title tag: "Service in City | Brand". H1 matches. 600 to 1,200 words of specific, local, useful content. Real photos. Embedded GBP map. Reviews on the page. Clear phone CTA at top on mobile.
- Technical baseline: mobile-first, loads under 2.5s LCP, HTTPS, clean URLs, XML sitemap submitted, LocalBusiness schema with same NAP as GBP, FAQ schema on service pages.
- Citations: get listed consistently on the top 30 directories plus local ones (Fredericksburg chamber, local news directories, Nextdoor, Yelp, BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor for contractors).
- Backlinks that matter locally: chamber of commerce, sponsorships (youth sports, church events, 5Ks), local news features, supplier or partner pages, local blogs. One real local link beats 100 spam links.
- AI search (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity) pulls from GBP, reviews, and pages that answer questions directly. Write FAQ sections that answer exactly what a homeowner asks. Consistency across the web is what gets a business cited.
- Never promise a ranking or a timeline. Say "most local sites see movement in 60 to 90 days with consistent reviews and content."

## Facebook and Instagram (organic)

- Facebook Groups are the best free lead channel in a town this size. Join every Fredericksburg / Stafford / Spotsylvania community, buy-sell, "recommendations", and small business group. Rules: read group rules first, no direct promo where banned, give value first (free site audits, free tips, answer "anyone know a good ___" threads by recommending real local businesses, which builds goodwill with those owners). Post 3 to 5 times per week across groups. Comment 10+ times daily.
- "ISO" (in search of) threads: when someone asks for a plumber/roofer/etc., note which businesses get tagged repeatedly and have no website. They are proven-demand leads. Add them to the list.
- Instagram: same content, Reels first. Local hashtags plus geotag Fredericksburg.
- Nextdoor: business page plus neighborly, non-salesy posts. Strong for home services.

## Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads

Creative decides 80% of results. Test 3 to 5 creatives, kill losers after 3 days or $30 spend with no lead. Expect local lead costs of roughly $8 to $40 depending on niche. Follow up with every lead within 5 minutes by phone or text or it is dead.
- Retargeting: video viewers and site visitors, small budget, proof content.
- Ad copy formula: call out the person and place ("Fredericksburg homeowners"), name the pain, show proof, single clear offer, one CTA.
- For Ron's own agency ads: offer-led ("Custom website, $1,000, live in 7 days, no templates") with a screenshot carousel of real work. Do not run these until there are at least 3 portfolio pieces.

## Cold Outreach — the only channel that pays this month
- Phone is the fastest. Email and Facebook/Instagram DM second. Text only with prior consent (TCPA). Email must follow CAN-SPAM (real name, real address, unsubscribe line).
- The offer must be dead simple and low risk. Default foot-in-the-door: "I already built a free mockup homepage for your business. Can I text you the link?" Build the mockup before or during the call using their GBP info. Then: "$1,000, live in a week, you pay when you love it" (or 50% up front once pipeline exists).
- Lead sourcing (ethical): Google Maps searches per niche and city, filter for no website, a Facebook-only website, a Wix/GoDaddy template, no mobile version, or under 10 reviews. Public business phone numbers only. Never scrape personal data.
- Expect: 100 dials, 25 to 35 conversations, 5 to 8 interested, 1 to 2 closes. Volume is the strategy.
- Every lead gets 5 to 7 touches over 3 weeks: call, voicemail, text (if consented), email with mockup, second call, DM, break-up message.
- Fast trust builders: show the mockup, show speed, offer to pay only when they love it, mention you are local and 10 minutes away, offer to meet in person.
